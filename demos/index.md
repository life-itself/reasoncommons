# Demos & experiments

Unpolished by design. These are here to show the idea in motion and to try things out, not as finished product. For the finished, readable work, see [the series](/explainers).

## Claim-tree annotation demo

[The four-step vision, animated](/claim-tree-annotation-demo/index.html) — take a claim, decompose it into a tree, bring in source documents, and map evidence onto the nodes. A scripted walk-through of what the tooling is aiming at.

## Constraints dashboard

[A read-only viewer](/dashboard/index.html) over a full Logical Thinking Process analysis of one organisation's goals and constraints — goal tree, current reality, conflicts, future reality, prerequisites. Several analyses sit behind a project picker. Prototype quality; the data model is real, the interface is minimal.

## Goal Aligner

[An experiment](/demos/goal-aligner/index.html) in turning a room's scattered goals into one tree everyone can sign off on. Standalone single-page app. (Formerly at `/alignment/`.)

## A talk that performs the method

[A five-act presentation](/talk/index.html) built so the audience experiences the Logical Thinking Process before hearing its name — each act opens on a question, and the reveal maps every act back to the tree that produced it.
