[The reasoning demonstration](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/index.html) is now a small site of commons instead of one long page. Each commons has an overview, a page for each of its six questions with diagrams drawn only from published connections, a view of every statement and connection, and a record of changes. Ours sits beside a clearly labelled fictional example, Willow Lane Primary, which shows what else a commons can hold: proposed and disputed reasoning, assumptions, open questions and reports from a trial.
