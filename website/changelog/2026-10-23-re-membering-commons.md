[Re-membering](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/re-membering/index.html) explores how we might care for the conditions that help us think and act together while preserving people’s agency, drawing on Margeret Heath’s Being-Becoming With the World, Talk 1. Its proposed reasoning includes source page references, possible benefits and harms, and open questions about consent, repair and tools; the AI reconstruction has no author endorsement or recorded trial results.
