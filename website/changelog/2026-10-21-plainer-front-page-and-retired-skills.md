The [home page](../README.md) and [About](../about.md) now open with what Reason Commons changes for a group: talk a goal or a problem through and come away with a shared picture of what you are trying to achieve and what you will do about it. The method underneath is still explained in [the Guide](/ltp), and the main button now leads to [the skill](/skills). Earlier framing that no longer describes the project has gone from the current pages, and a site check stops it coming back.

Two skills built for the retired Reason Commons app, `ltp-project` and `ltp-visualize`, are retired too; [`reason-commons`](/skills) is the skill to install.
