When you ask the [`reason-commons` skill](https://github.com/life-itself/reasoncommons/blob/main/skills/reason-commons/SKILL.md) to show a tree, it now draws the real diagram in the chat, with drag to pan and buttons, pinch or ⌘/Ctrl+scroll to zoom, instead of retelling the tree as a list of chains. The diagram, its node counts and the "Not shown" line are generated straight from the saved reasoning, so smaller models such as Haiku get them right too.
