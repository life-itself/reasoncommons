After looking at an earlier version of the map, Leif Eriksson pointed out that it missed a central theme of his work: movements that succeed within an existing order can be reshaped by the very capabilities that brought them success. [Gandhi 2.0](/demos/reason-commons-in-use/gandhi-2-0/conflict.html#view-k6) now includes it. The goal's six items are treated as success factors, and the fifth now rests on two conditions: surviving in selection between institutions, and keeping selection within them faithful to what they exist for. A sixth conflict sets these against each other, and a new change sets out Leif's hypothesis that human–AI collaboration and institutional design might alter the selection pressures themselves. Each new idea links to his posts in the thread. It all remains proposed and worded by the AI, and Leif has not yet reviewed the new wording.
