In the Claude apps, the [`reason-commons` skill](https://github.com/life-itself/reasoncommons/blob/main/skills/reason-commons/SKILL.md) now draws each turn's change-status diagram as a rendered Mermaid flowchart inside the conversation, with proposed links dashed and accepted, proposed and disputed nodes outlined in different colours. Terminals and other plain-text chats keep the ASCII diagrams.
