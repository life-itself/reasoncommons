Second Renaissance thread: thinking-process trees · supplied text · 22 October 2026

Supplied earlier analysis and presentation notes, based on a reported 32-post forum thread. Its throughput and governance-capacity proposals are hypotheses for a bounded case. The later PDF broadens the programme and distinguishes local improvements from a durable change in institutional selection. The draft introductory forum post was not sent. No original forum URL or manuscript excerpt is published as if independently verified.

The broader programme

The later analysis covers reflective human development, trustworthy institutions, the Laboratory, Forge/model adaptation, Vocal Presence, federation, material and energy services, and enforceable security. It separates philosophical hypotheses from institutional mechanisms and distinguishes local success from a durable shift in institutional selection. This compact starting map leaves those branches as open review work.

What counts as progress?

The earlier analysis proposes measuring practices adopted and retained in viable institutions that remain faithful to their purpose. The measures, system boundary, observation period and protected conditions still need agreement. Essays and conversation alone do not establish that outcome.

What “accepted for now” means

Named people are willing to build on a statement or connection for now. It records their judgement at the time. It does not establish truth, and it does not mean everyone agrees: each item names exactly who accepted it.

Contributed is not the same as accepted

Each item records who contributed the idea and, separately, who accepted it. Ideas suggested by the AI are attributed to the AI, and only people can accept anything.

What isn't here

The original forum posts, manuscript and source documents are not reproduced here. No participant endorsement, trial owner, budget, baseline, numerical target, feedback commitment or observed result is recorded. The map does not establish one global constraint.
