Pulling apart

Useful capacity, without becoming captive

A community needs enough capability to endure. It also needs to keep essential functions running when a provider, power supply or AI fails. Does that really require choosing between dependence on AI and doing without it?

A possible change

Try conflict guidance that members can question and leave

The proposal is to test useful guidance while members retain control, access to human help and competence without the tool. Confidentiality, fair grievance handling and independent comparison are part of the design.

ChangeProposedTest member-controlled AI conflict guidance with voluntary use, human support, a workable exit and competence checked without AI.Proposed by AI assistantChangeProposedMembers establish confidentiality, fair grievance handling and independent comparison before live use.Proposed by AI assistant

The question to test

What would change our minds?

Do destructive conflict costs and unnecessary escalation fall, while fair treatment, independent competence and member influence remain? Compare AI guidance with optional-AI and human approaches.

Expected effectProposedDestructive conflict costs and unnecessary hierarchical intervention fall while fair treatment, independent competence and member influence are retained.Proposed by AI assistantPossible harmProposedConflict guidance may suppress justified grievances even when satisfaction improves.Proposed by AI assistant

Fewer complaints or higher satisfaction would not settle it. Costs may have moved elsewhere, or justified grievances may have been silenced. Agree the comparison and the results that would defeat the claim before starting.

A local success would still leave a larger question open: do small democratic groups gain enough relative to hierarchies to change which institutions survive? The supplied analyses do not establish that step.

Choose one willing host and one function

No trial is recorded here. The first task is to establish a bounded case, check what already operates, and agree measures, protections, responsibilities and resources with the people affected. A missing detail in an analysis does not prove that a host lacks it.
