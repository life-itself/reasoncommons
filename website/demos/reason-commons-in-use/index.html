A demonstration of Reason Commons

Common goals,
reasoned in the open.

A commons is a goal and the reasoning a group builds around it: what it depends on, what stands in the way, where needs pull apart, and what the group decides to do. Groups build a commons in ordinary conversation with an AI, using the Reason Commons skill. This site is where anyone can read one.

Read our commons →See the worked example

Every commons answers six questions

  1. 1What are we trying to achieve?The goal, and what has to be true for it.
  2. 2What stands in the way?What is happening now, and why.
  3. 3Where do needs pull two ways?Two needs that both serve one objective, and positions that rule each other out.
  4. 4What will we change?The change, and what it should lead to.
  5. 5What has to happen first?What stands in the way of the change, and what gets past it.
  6. 6Who does what?Actions, and what each should achieve.

Each answer sets up the next.

How a commons gets made

The site is read-only. All the thinking happens in conversation, where the skill keeps the record.

  1. 1

    Talk it through

    A group works on a shared goal in ordinary conversations with an AI. The skill turns what people say into statements and connections, and keeps track of who said what.

  2. 2

    Accept what you can build on

    Named people accept statements and connections for now. Proposals stay marked as proposals, and objections are kept as disputes.

  3. 3

    Publish a selection

    The group decides what to make public. Each release keeps earlier wording, so anyone can see how the thinking changed.

  4. 4

    Read, then respond

    Anyone can read a commons here. To add to one, copy a statement's brief into your own AI. Nothing is posted from this site.
