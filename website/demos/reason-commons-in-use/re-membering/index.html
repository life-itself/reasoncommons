What has to be true

Shared support with room to choose

The proposed goal depends on voluntary participation, the ability to notice strain and repair, and tools whose purposes people can question. These conditions are an AI interpretation of the talk, awaiting review.

What stands in the way

The body as a private machine

The talks ask what is lost when the body is treated as something to optimise alone. Two other proposed chains concern fixed targets with unintended harms, and technologies whose purposes users did not choose.

A possible change

Arrive together, without owing the room your calm

The script offers a short, optional settling practice. This map puts the invitation beside its conditions and risk: some may feel more able to be with others, while some may feel pressure or less safe. No practice has been tried or evaluated here.

Beyond a single exercise

Listen, retune, and question the tools

The wider proposal is a way of attending: act, notice how bodies, people and environments respond, and change the next move. Ask whether the tools shaping the setting widen people’s choices and whose purposes they serve.

The argument behind the practice

Body, tools and a wider world

The talk travels through embodied cognition, predictive processing and extended minds toward a somatic commons. It distinguishes evidence for coupling, contested extension and a philosophical commitment to participation. This practical reconstruction does not require agreement that tools constitute minds. A willing group, any conflict, prerequisites, assigned actions and participant assessment still need to be established.
