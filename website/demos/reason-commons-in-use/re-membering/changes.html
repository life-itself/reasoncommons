    18 statements and 11 connections
    People tend the conditions for thinking and acting together while retaining agency.Participation in shared practices is safe, voluntary and open to withdrawal.People can notice strain and repair their shared rhythms.Tools and environments support purposes that people can question and change.The body is treated as a private machine to optimise and repair alone.Individuals carry a regulation burden shaped by relationships and surroundings.Fixed targets are pursued without attending to the wider system's responses.An intervention can meet its target while causing unintended harm elsewhere.Technologies can shape attention around purposes people have not chosen.People's capacity to choose and learn can be narrowed.Offer shared rhythms of rest and meeting that participants can adapt or leave.Agree how people can decline, raise harm and seek repair before a shared practice.Shared settling may help some participants feel more able to be with others.A shared practice may create pressure or make some participants feel less safe.Adjust actions by listening to bodies, people and environments.Feedback can change what is tried next instead of preserving a fixed target.Assess tools by whether they widen choices and whose purposes they serve.Tools can support chosen purposes while remaining open to question.and 11 connections
