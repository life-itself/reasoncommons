Part 1 of 3

Offer a practice that people can leave

Shared rhythms and consent arrangements act together in the proposed benefit. Pressure and feeling less safe remain possible harms; an opt-out is not proof that pressure has been prevented.

Let responses change the next move

Part 3 of 3

Ask whether a tool widens choices

Assessment is meant to support chosen purposes, but naming a purpose does not itself change a tool’s design or ownership. The connection remains a hypothesis.
