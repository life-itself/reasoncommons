# Independent critique — script v1

**Verdict:** not "strong with minor issues." One excellent section and one excellent closing
beat, carried by three passages that don't pull their weight and a closing claim that
outruns the evidence.

## The comprehension bug

The retired sixth tool ("a step-by-step implementation map") sits **directly after** the
section about implementation ("what do we do on Monday, and in what order?"). The piece never
distinguishes them. A reader not already tracking Dettmer's taxonomy will think the tool just
described is the one that got cut — at precisely the credibility-building moment that was
supposed to be the most trustworthy beat in the piece.

Also: "You should trust the remaining five more, not less, for that" tells the reader what to
conclude — ironic in a series whose Part 2 argues that unearned assertions are what should
get challenged. The sentence doesn't survive its own eight questions.

## Other findings

- **Section 5 has zero clinic content.** "Money, skills, permissions, sequence" could be lifted into any project-management deck. The section most in need of the running example is the one that drops it.
- **Section 1 never states the clinic's goal.** We're told a goal document matters; we never see what it says.
- **The recap block is a manual restating a manual.** Five parallel noun phrases, immediately after five sections meant to make them unnecessary.
- **"A group whose thinking accumulates" is unearned.** Nothing in the series shows the tree persisting — Part 2 puts it "on the wall", which reads as ephemeral. The March line asserts institutional memory the series never demonstrates.
- **Every section uses the identical scaffold** — bold question, setup, "so" pivot, instruction, clinic tie-back. Five times. Nothing varies the rhythm.
- **"Nero syndrome: fiddling while Rome burns. Rearranging deck chairs"** — two dead clichés back to back.
- **The music-notation analogy doesn't hold** — notation reproduces a fixed performance; a claim tree is contested and mutable.
- **Maria vanishes.** She is the emotional centre of Part 1 and never returns. The series resolves its idea but not its people.
- **"That is the actual promise"** is sales copy; **"Claims get addresses"** bolded reads as a slide.

## Protect in revision

"You cannot have a useful argument about a gap when nobody has agreed where the far side
is." · "They aren't four problems. They are four fingerprints." · "Not a personality clash —
a structural one, where the thing hurting you is protecting something else you also need." ·
"Break one assumption and the conflict doesn't get resolved. It stops existing." · "This is
where a surprising number of good ideas quietly die, on paper, for free." · "Somebody else
thinks that's wrong. / For the first time, they can find out."
