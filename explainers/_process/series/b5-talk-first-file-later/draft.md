# Draft — B5, *Talk first, file later*

Series B, chapter 5 of 5. Bead `reasoncommons-bnl.22`. Prose only, no visual direction. Follows [B4](../b4-why-nobody-does-the-filing/script.md), which ends on Maren: nothing is asked of her that was not asked before.

---

**Key claim:** AI reads the conversation afterwards and proposes where each point belongs; people decide what goes in.

---

Here is the run.

Nine people contributed to a session on the Second Renaissance's strategy, in the way people contribute to sessions: a paragraph each, in their own words, nobody naming a node or learning a notation. Afterwards we gave those paragraphs and the movement's real tree to a model and asked it, for each one, what the person was asserting and where in the tree it belongs.

Take Maya's. She wrote that she is sympathetic to the big civilizational goal but not sure it helps the group make choices — "transform civilization" is broad enough to justify almost anything, and she would want a nearer-term statement of success she could measure progress against.

The reading came back as a challenge to the root claim: *the goal is too broad to discriminate between courses of action, so it cannot do the work a goal is supposed to do for this group*. Not that the goal is wrong — that it cannot rule anything out. That is a specific objection, at a specific address, and anybody can now agree or disagree with it there.

It also came back with a second reading, marked as the less likely one: Maya might instead be proposing an intermediate goal under the root, sustaining a few local communities that live the values over time. She does mention that. The model's judgement was that she offers it as an illustration of what is missing rather than as a proposal — and it says so, rather than choosing silently.

Across the nine, six readings came back high confidence and three medium. Some are challenges to a claim, some to a link, some propose a new condition.

And one came back unplaced. Ana asked where art, celebration, grief ritual, music, shared meals and beauty fit — not practice exactly, not outreach exactly, but often what makes a culture feel alive enough that people want to belong to it. The nearest address was the condition about people durably embodying practices. Filing it there would have made celebration and grief instrumental, a means to adoption, which is close to the reading Ana was wary of. So the model declined to place it, and said why: the model has no category for what a culture is like to belong to, as distinct from what it asks of people.

That is the useful failure. A contribution the tree cannot hold is not noise; it is the tree telling you where it is thin.

**Nothing in that run was merged. The machine proposes; people decide what goes in.** For a technical reader: it is pull requests for discourse, with the same review step and the same right to say no.

And it runs the other way too. A tree that knows what it holds can tell the next person arriving that this ground has been covered, and point them at the thread where it was.

None of this is finished. The trees are provisional and wrong in places, the reader is a prototype, and the merge rules are a draft. The contributors in this run were rehearsal, not the room.

What is true is that the cost of contributing is back to what it always was — writing a sentence you mean — and the filing still gets done. So: find the claim you disagree with. Or find the one nobody is working on.
