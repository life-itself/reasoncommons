<p class="ch-eyebrow"><a href="../">Where does the thinking go?</a> · Chapter 2 of 5</p>

<p class="ch-lede">Forums file by date and papers file by paragraph, so a single claim has no address — and what you can't point at, you can't improve.</p>

So where was Maren's comment supposed to go?

The forum files things by date. That is the only question it can answer: what was said, and when. It cannot tell you what the movement currently thinks about place, or which of its commitments her comment bears on.

Writing it all up properly doesn't fix this either. The movement has six or seven white papers, close to a hundred thousand words, and they are careful and good. But a claim buried in a paragraph on page forty has no address. You cannot point at it. And if you cannot point at it, you cannot disagree with it precisely, or improve it, or say that the version on page forty and the version in the other paper are not the same claim.

A vague objection to a long document is one nobody can act on, so in the end nobody makes it. Maren did not go quiet because she stopped caring. She went quiet because there was no move available to her that would have changed anything.

**That means the most valuable thing a newcomer brings — a specific suggestion, or a specific disagreement — is exactly the thing we are least able to receive.**

It is worth being precise about what is missing, because it is not enthusiasm and it is not rigour. It is an address: a place a claim lives, that other claims can refer to, that you can watch change over time.
