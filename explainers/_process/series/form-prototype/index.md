# Where does the thinking go?

<p class="ch-lede">Five short chapters on why a group's good conversations don't add up, and what would have to be true for them to.</p>

Most groups are not short of intelligence, goodwill or ideas. They are short of a way to integrate them, so the same good conversation happens again every few months and nothing the group holds in common changes. This series follows one contributor's suggestion through a real movement's forum, and works out what was missing — a place for a claim to live — and what makes giving every claim one affordable now.

By the end you should be able to say why structure is the thing that is missing, what a tree of claims is, why nobody wants to do the filing, and what changes when the filing no longer has to be done by the person contributing.

<ol class="ch-toc ch-toc-lg">
<li><a href="01-same-conversation/"><span class="ch-toc-title">The same conversation, again and again</span><span class="ch-toc-teaser">Groups keep having the same good conversation, because what one person learns compounds but what the group agrees keeps resetting to zero.</span></a></li>
<li><a href="02-nowhere-to-put-it/"><span class="ch-toc-title">Nowhere to put it</span><span class="ch-toc-teaser">Forums file by date and papers file by paragraph, so a single claim has no address — and what you can't point at, you can't improve.</span></a></li>
<li><a href="03-give-every-claim-an-address/"><span class="ch-toc-title">Give every claim an address</span><span class="ch-toc-teaser">A tree of claims gives every claim, and every link between them, a place you can point to — so Maren's suggestion has somewhere to land.</span></a></li>
