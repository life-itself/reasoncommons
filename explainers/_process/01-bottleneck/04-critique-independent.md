# Independent critique — script v2

**Verdict:** competent persuasion with a real, checkable factual error at the exact spot
where the piece asks the reader to trust its arithmetic; and one rhetorical device used so
often it reads as a formula executing itself.

## The load-bearing defect

> "Not eighty-five, which is what its five stations could manage between them."

Capacities are 60/45/20/50/40. Sum = 215. Not 85, not 195 (sum excluding the doctor), not
an average, not 40 (the next binding constraint). **No combination yields 85.** Any reader
who does the check the piece is daring them to do gets a number that doesn't match, at the
exact moment the piece claims precision as its authority.

Also: "Four of the five can handle more than twice what the doctor can" — twice 20 is 40,
and follow-up is *exactly* 40. Only three are strictly more than double.

Also: the 20→28 gain is asserted, dressed as computed. No hours or minutes-per-patient are
given from which 28 could be derived.

## Other findings

- **The generalisation is never named as a leap.** The serial-pipeline arithmetic is proven; the extension to organisations, software teams and families is analogy presented with identical confidence. Most white-collar work has parallel paths, rework loops and batching. The piece concedes that *identifying* the constraint is hard but never that the underlying flow model is a simplification.
- **The exploit step is unstitched.** "Put them anywhere but here does nothing" and "move two hours off her desk" are the same move — reallocating toward the constraint — but presented as a fresh insight.
- **The doctor has no name and no detail** despite being the answer to the entire piece.
- **The antithesis tic** — "It's not X, it's Y" — used five times. The single most recognisable tic of both LLM prose and management-book writing.
- **The Gardner citation** is the only outside authority in a piece that otherwise earns everything itself.
- **Momentum sags** at the team/parent passage: two undeveloped analogies dropped in after three developed characters.
- **Ends on pure diagnosis** with no signal the problem is solvable — risky for a piece that must work standalone.

## Protect in revision

"They are all wrong." · "She is, visibly and unarguably, the hardest-working person in the
building. / She is also irrelevant to how many people this clinic helps." · "The other forty
go home." · "frustrated sitting down instead of standing up" · "A waiting room is not
output. It is forty afternoons, spent." · "A bottleneck rule has no pile." · the closing
callback to Maria.
