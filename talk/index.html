Willingness is not the scarce resource. Somewhere for willingness to land is.

The point is not that discussion is bad.

Groups are surprisingly bad at
accumulating reasoning — and at
converting it into coordinated action.

Imagine a conversation where

  • useful insights accumulate rather than disappear

  • new contributions connect to what has already been learned

  • disagreements become explicit rather than recurring indefinitely

  • you can see what is currently blocking progress

  • offers of effort have somewhere concrete to go

  • action stays traceable to the reasoning that motivated it

The destination

A group gets smarter over time,
and its thinking increasingly
results in action.

Our hypothesis

Collective intelligence is not primarily a matter of having more intelligent people, or more conversation.

It is about the process through which reasoning accumulates, bottlenecks become visible, and action follows.

Both of these are true

What people want

Conversation stays natural and open.

Nobody wants every message turned into formal logic. People want spontaneity, ambiguity, intuition, exploration.

What accumulation needs

Reasoning becomes structured enough to build on.

If everything stays conversational, the important ideas disappear into chronology.

02 We defined the goal — groups that get smarter and act. Goal Tree
