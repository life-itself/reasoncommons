Always in this order

  1. 1 Where does it fit?
  2. 2 Gap or disagreement?
  3. 3 So what, and who owns it?

Question one is the easy part, and it is the only part a machine does well. Two and three are the group’s work.

The three questions

This slide stays up while you walk one or two contributions through. Each number lights as you reach it.

1 — where does it fit? The tree proposes a location. Easy part.
2 — gap or disagreement? Is it under a condition nobody is holding, or in tension with a link? Now it is doing work on the group’s reasoning.
3 — so what? If the room broadly agrees: what is the next action, and who might own it?

If the placement is weak, say so, place it by hand, and move on. A wrong first guess that a person corrects in five seconds is still the system working.
