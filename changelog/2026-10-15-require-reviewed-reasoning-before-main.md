Changes to Reason Commons now require a pull request, a passing reasoning check and an independent review before reaching the live branch, so the proposed contribution to the project's goal is visible for review. [The rules apply to maintainers and bots too](https://github.com/life-itself/reasoncommons/rules/25147483).
