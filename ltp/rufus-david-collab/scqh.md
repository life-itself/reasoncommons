# Rufus and David's collaboration — SCQH

Source: the 2026-09-25 conversation (transcript kept uncommitted in `drafts/`). Tree: [rufus-david-collab.ltp.yaml](rufus-david-collab.ltp.yaml), with every judgment call in the [report](rufus-david-collab.report.md). Question and goal agreed by Rufus and David on 2026-09-25.

## Situation

Rufus and David have collaborated for three months out of a shared interest in thinking and collaborating better together, which they connect to the Logical Thinking Process made accessible through AI. They have built several websites and apps, have a fairly good "why now" story, and AI-supported sense-making is probably of real interest to philanthropic funders. A presentation of the work got a mixed reception.

## Complication

They have no resources beyond their own time, have not applied for funding and have no customers. They have not decided between philanthropic funding and a startup, whether either wants a business rather than a Second Renaissance project, or which of several compatible goals is the real one. Licensing of the software is unclear and ownership has never been discussed. They are unclear what the product should look like, do not know who the users are, have no app anyone uses (themselves included), and have seen no sign of product-market fit.

## Question

What form should our collaboration take over the next six months, and what would make it worth continuing?

## Goal

The Logical Thinking Process, made accessible through AI, is used in the world to help people think and act better together.

Critical success factors:

- The work is resourced beyond Rufus and David's spare time.
- Real people use the tool, with Second Renaissance as the first user and testbed.
- The product is something people can picture themselves using.
- Rufus and David have a clear agreement on ownership, licensing and commitment.

Money, from grants or revenue, is a means to resourcing, not the goal.
