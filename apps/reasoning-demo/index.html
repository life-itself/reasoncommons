A conversation that
you can build on.

Use Reason Commons in an AI conversation to work through your own goal or problem. The AI helps structure the thinking and keep the record; people decide what to accept or challenge.

Try it on your own question ↗
  1. 01

    Bring a real question

    Start with what you are trying to achieve and what makes it difficult.

  2. 02

    Make the reasoning visible

    Inspect the statements, their connections, and who contributed them.

  3. 03

    Review it together

    Accept a fragment for now, challenge a connection, or keep it open.

  4. 04

    Return to the record

    Continue from what was saved, and review the reasoning as you learn.
