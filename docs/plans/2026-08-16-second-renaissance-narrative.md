# Plan — Second Renaissance explainer, narrative restart

## Position

Sibling to the [Thinking in Systems trilogy](https://github.com/life-itself/reasoncommons/issues/6),
in parallel with it rather than after it — carried over from #7, nothing here
contradicts it. Deliberately unnumbered
(`explainers/second-renaissance/index.html`), so nothing implies a required
position in a sequence. Should read well either way round: someone who has
read the trilogy arrives with context; someone handed only this link, cold,
still gets a complete piece.

This plan replaces #7's *narrative* brief. #7's original job description was,
in effect, "rewrite `story/draft.md` as scrollable — same material, better
form." That premise is dropped (see Why this exists). #7's process,
sourcing, and pipeline decisions still apply and are restated below rather
than re-litigated.

**Scope boundary.** This plan covers one piece: the accessible on-ramp. The
deeper question of how a group would actually define and debate progress —
including the real June "define throughput" episode — is out of scope here,
noted as a possible separate, later piece. Don't let this plan grow to cover
two deliverables.

## Why this exists

We don't have a convincing story for why the Logical Thinking Process /
claim trees matter to Second Renaissance. This piece is that story.

The current built page (`03-script-v2.md`, shipped in #7) doesn't tell it.
It's a condensed retelling of `story/draft.md`'s "define throughput"
episode — same quotes, same beats, tightened prose. That premise assumes a
reader already immersed in the trees and the LTP jargon; for a cold SR
reader it's a level down from where the piece needs to start, and "what is
throughput, and why does that sound like a factory" is the reaction it
gets before the actual argument can land.

## The thesis: one question, two answers

Not two coequal motivations running in parallel — one thesis, answered two
ways, interwoven rather than presented as separate sections.

**Answer A — primary.** A structured, evolving way of thinking helps Second
Renaissance actually align as a project: refine and organize a rigorous
theory of change that can evolve over time, integrate new people and new
ideas, organize research and work around the theory of change, assess
progress against it. Without something like this, the default outcome is
fragmentation — everyone pursuing their own version of the work, with little
shared sense of direction.

**Answer B — secondary, complementary.** The same structure lets ordinary
forum discourse and sense-making accumulate instead of repeating itself —
new people showing up, the same ground re-covered, nothing aggregating —
via an AI bridge that nobody has to learn LTP to benefit from. Write this
aspirationally: regardless of what's actually built and working today in
any existing prototype, describe what becomes possible. This is the SR-scale
instance of a general point already in `motivation.md`: "discussion scatters
across forum posts and never compounds into shared understanding" /
"cathedral building instead of scattered noise."

## Opening

Leads on Answer B's on-ramp into Answer A, not on either in isolation:
Second Renaissance is an ambitious, largely volunteer-run project with an
evolving thesis — how do new people and new ideas actually join in and
cohere with the existing work? That question opens naturally into the
forum's actual texture (Answer B), which opens into why that requires
structure and not just goodwill (Answer A).

Explicitly not the opening: "what does it mean to make progress on Second
Renaissance" — this is itself a level down from the real question, not the
top-level hook. And not the word "throughput," or the real June episode —
both presuppose a reader already inside the trees.

## Ending

No single CTA is locked in. The piece should land both:

1. I can see how to get involved and align myself with the project (Answer A).
2. I can see how my own sense-making and discourse contributes to refining
   the thesis (Answer B).

A concrete close such as "go look at the trees, find your view in them" is
a candidate, not committed — may or may not converge into one beat once
drafted.

## Audience

Any Second Renaissance community member — forum participants generally, not
just the core/research team. Has to work cold: no LTP background assumed,
per #7's original brief on this point.

## Appendix — brainstorming FAQ

Distilled from the questions asked while shaping this plan, in the order
asked.

**What in the current draft is worth keeping?**
Unknown yet — don't decide before the new brief is settled. Assess reuse
once a new stage-1 script exists, not now.

**Who is this piece for?**
Any SR community member, not just the core/research team. Has to work cold.

**Is this two motivations, or one thesis with two answers?**
One thesis — "why does LTP matter to Second Renaissance" — answered two
ways. Answer A (structured alignment / evolving theory of change /
organizing work) is primary. Answer B (forum discourse accumulating instead
of repeating) is secondary and complementary, interwoven rather than a
separate section.

**Where does the existing point about people not self-adopting annotation
live?**
It's a general point, not SR-specific, already in `motivation.md`:
"discussion scatters across forum posts and never compounds into shared
understanding." `claim-tree-annotation.md` / the annotation demo are the
related process material, not the source of this specific point.

**Should the AI forum-bridge be written as existing or aspirational?**
Aspirational, regardless of what's actually built and working today.

**Should the real "define throughput" episode (Robert/David, June) be used?**
Not as this piece's spine, and not in the cold open — it already presumes
the reader is deep in LTP jargon and trees. If used at all, it belongs much
later, heavily reframed in plain language, or better, in a separate deeper
piece — out of scope for this plan.

**How should the piece open?**
Not "what does progress mean" — that's itself a level down from the real
question. The real top-level hook, chosen from several candidates named
(refining a rigorous evolving thesis; new people/ideas joining in; SR as
sense-making in service of coherence): **how do new people and new ideas
actually join in and cohere with the existing work.**

**What should the ending ask of the reader?**
Depends on how the piece evolves — don't force one CTA. But land both
answers: I can see how to get involved/align myself with the project, and I
can see how my sense-making/discourse contributes to refining the thesis.

**Does this plan cover the deeper progress/throughput material too, or just
the intro?**
Just the intro piece. The deeper material is a separate, later, out-of-scope
piece.
