# Positioning (v2)

Internal. Derived from [brand-narrative.md](brand-narrative.md). Revised from v1 after four red-team critiques ([redteam/](redteam/)) and Rufus's corrections. One argument, not a scoring table.

## The line

**Keep it human. Make it add up.**

Under it: **Every claim gets an address.**

Why this one. Every group that tries to think together hits the same trade: keep it human (quick, exploratory, nobody filling in forms) or make it add up (structure, or the good parts dissolve). The talk names that dilemma; this line deletes the "or". That is the transformation, said as a promise of capability rather than another "your thinking is remembered" line (the whole accumulate / compound / remember family is dropped — it read as the rejected line in a new coat). It is true of the skill today: you talk normally and come out with a structure. Runner-up, for contexts about disagreement: **Agree on the record. Disagree precisely.**

## What we say is true today

Talk a goal, a problem or a plan through with an AI, alone or as a group, and come out with a shared tree: what you are trying to achieve, what has to be true, what is in the way, and what you will do about it. Every claim has an address and a name on it; nothing counts as agreed until people accept it; disagreements are marked where they are, not re-argued from scratch. It runs today as a skill in Claude Code or Codex. We use it on our own work.

## Where it is going (always marked "next")

Point the same machinery at the conversation a group already has — a forum, a Discord — and have it propose, after the fact, where each contribution lands on the tree: supports this, challenges that, nowhere to put it yet. People decide what merges. Pull requests for discourse. We have prototyped annotating discourse live; Discord integration is next. The vision may run a little ahead of what has shipped — say "we've prototyped", not "you can".

## Why now

The method works and almost nobody uses it. It comes from Goldratt's Theory of Constraints — developed from the 1970s, published in *The Goal* (1984) — and its Thinking Processes, codified by Dettmer as the Logical Thinking Process. It asks a lot: an expert facilitator, and the discipline of testing every cause-and-effect link across half a dozen linked diagrams. So it lived inside large organisations that could pay for it — so few people used it — so few people knew it — so it never became practice in the communities, movements and public debates that need it most. AI breaks that loop: it does the drafting, holds the structure and keeps it current, so the people can do the judging.

## Positioning statement

For groups that share a goal and keep losing what they work out — communities, movements, research circles, small organisations — Reason Commons is an open AI skill that turns a conversation into a shared, lasting tree of what the group thinks and what it will do, with every claim attributed and nothing agreed until people accept it. Unlike meeting summaries, one-off AI syntheses (Talk to the City), opinion mapping (Polis) or hand-built debate maps (Kialo), the tree persists, is versioned, and gets better each time the group comes back to it.

## Value propositions

| Audience | Promise | Proof today | One action |
|---|---|---|---|
| People who'd run it (facilitators, stewards, AI-native builders) | Run a group's thinking through conversation; leave with an attributed, accepted tree and a diagram of what changed. | The skill as shipped; our own public working file; our PRs must cite the reasoning they serve. | Install the skill and try one prompt. Needs Claude Code or Codex. |
| Mission-aligned newcomers | Stop having the same argument twice. | A real before/after: messy material next to the tree made from it, each claim linked to its source. Second Renaissance tree of change. | Look at a real tree — no install. |
| Funders and partners | A cheap, open route to structured collective reasoning for groups that could never afford it, built in the open. | Repo, changelog, self-use. No outside-group outcome yet. | Fund a first pilot with a named community and an outcome measure. |
| LTP / TOC practitioners | A way to keep a client's trees alive between your sessions, and to get the method into communities. | The skill's tree types and challenge handling; the guide and glossary. | Run it on a tree you already keep; tell us where it's wrong. |

Two further value propositions, from the [idea map](brand-narrative.md#the-idea-map):

- **Trees → AI** (AI crowd, funders): tree-thinking is the missing structure for humans and AI thinking and planning together. AI proposes, people accept, and the work lives in one structure — not in sprawling chat threads and dozens of markdown files that become a morass as a project grows. Proof today: the skill, and our own collaboration file.
- **Tree → discourse** (community stewards): the tree maps the conversation — repetition, agreement, gaps, and "this thread is about claim X from six months ago". Status: specified, not yet built; say "next".

Practitioners are allies, not the thing being replaced: never "you no longer need a consultant".

## Tone

Plain, not clever. Concrete before visionary — a real tree before any sentence about civilisation. Inviting, not asserting — "accepted for now", not "true". Honest about status: today / next / the aim, in those words, every time. One person with conviction, in the talk's voice — not a committee.

## Corrections carried from the red team

- History: anchor on the Theory of Constraints (developed from the 1970s, *The Goal* 1984), which makes "forty years" fair; the Thinking Processes came later, and the LTP name with Dettmer (2007). Not "six trees": five trees plus the Evaporating Cloud — say "half a dozen linked diagrams".
- Don't claim the Categories of Legitimate Reservation unless the skill implements them.
- Soften and attribute the cost figure ("expert facilitators at consultancy rates"), or source it.
- Drop "addressable", "pull request" and "skill" from newcomer-facing lines; "theory of change" is a phrase movement people already use.
- Add Talk to the City and meeting summarisers to the competitive set; don't overstate what Polis lacks.
- "No app" is settled: the app is retired; the offer is skills plus integrations.
