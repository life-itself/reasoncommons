## Ranked objections and revisions

1. **The original opening contradicts its daily demand.** Sixty already waiting at noon, plus patients already seen, cannot straightforwardly mean sixty attempts over the day. Revision: the cold open says the queue is outside by noon; the early narrative separately states sixty attempts per typical working day and twenty completed consultations. Keep sixty as a case quantity without pretending it is a simultaneous headcount.
2. **The hiring comparison assumes a job definition.** A front-desk hire could in fact provide preparation support and improve clinical capacity. Revision: specify a hire used only to speed check-in, then leave the role of the eventual hire open. The useful distinction is the capacity changed, not the person's job title.
3. **Part three still needs to explain ten versus eighteen.** Revision: explicitly distinguish booked slot length from the observed average consultation before using the appointment conflict. Do not assert preparation closes the whole gap.
4. **Do not manufacture unanimous endorsement.** Revision: use the same complete goal in the main narrative, conversation and saved fragment, with Sam as its named endorser. Explain provisional/named acceptance once; remove repeated defensive explanations from scene endings.
5. **The first draft still generalized root-cause convergence.** Revision: ask what could produce each symptom and what evidence supports the connection. Preserve alternatives instead of promising one or two causes.
6. **Some narrative sentences repeat the artifact's caution.** Revision: shorten scene consequences to the useful change. Keep qualifications on predictions and the original evidence limits; do not repeat a disclaimer after every bubble.
7. **The source-note edit accidentally moved attribution.** Revision: distinguish the fictional funding prediction from Dettmer's general observation about unchecked diagrams. The new example is not credited to the book.
8. **Graphics could silently contradict the revised story.** Required build gate: remove the categorical medical prediction from both diagram layouts, change the migration drawing to triage and a shared limit, render candidate causes/predictions as such, remove the guaranteed future loop, and change the obstacle chain from immediate hiring to a covered trial. Static alternatives must carry the same meaning.
9. **An authentic trace can become another polished illustration.** Required evidence gate: record actual operations and exact outputs, label assistant-authored inputs, retain before/challenge/after states, and identify a fresh-process read as a persistence check rather than a fresh model's reasoning. Do not fabricate a human reply, a clinical result or an independent test.
10. **More material can make a better argument feel slower.** Required build gate: remove the aging-parent detour and sixth-tool body diversion, eliminate message-by-message scroll padding, shorten redundant holds, and compare the entire old story-plus-conversation against the new source. Check actual reading burden on a phone; do not claim a word-count reduction alone establishes better pacing.
