# Editorial critique of the revised margin

An adversarial editorial pass by the implementing assistant, not an independent reader study. Reviewed after saving the revised narrative and before mapping or implementation. Scope: `explainer-margin-pacing-2026-10-20` revision 1.

The continuous clinic, paper-and-ink register and role-specific devices are working. Keep them. The margin earns its space when an affected person contributes something a manager could miss, or when the record keeps a disagreement available for examination.

## Ranked faults to prevent

1. **Stealing the discovery.** No diagnosis or conversation during the station introductions. Reveal capacities only as the story does. The explicit skill invitation follows the forty unseen people.
2. **Two essays competing.** One short idea per arrival. Show at most the newest message and its predecessor; reverse scroll restores earlier exchanges. No independently scrolling transcript.
3. **Mistaking animation for pacing.** A scroll beat determines the available messages. Waiting must never reveal the next one. A notification gets its own beat before the thread opens.
4. **Capturing private thinking too soon.** Part two must keep explanations private until the externalisation passage. The questions scaffold scrutiny rather than arriving after a finished diagnosis.
5. **The AI deciding the answer.** Keep authors, proposed/disputed standings, human acceptance and conditional predictions. Mark AI inferences as suggestions. Sam never stands for everyone else.
6. **Device ornament shrinking the words.** Preserve the device and role, but make the current message readable. On narrow screens use a compact lower margin with the drawing above it, rather than inserting a second article between chapters.
7. **Implying proactive messages.** Notifications come from colleagues. Captured phone contributions show capture mode. Label the chat prototype at the device; installation remains about the existing skill.

The visual mapping must assign each message its own beat, keep the cast visible, distinguish the skill invitation, restore prior knowledge when scrolling up and preserve information in reduced motion. Browser checks can establish these properties. Reader comprehension and product interest remain untested.
