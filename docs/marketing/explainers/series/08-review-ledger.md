## Case ledger: declared fictional assumptions

Ashfield is a simplified, invented clinic, not a deployment or a healthcare recommendation. The five-station model assumes every modeled visit passes through each station, with stable demand and capacities. Real routes, variability and clinical decisions are more complicated. The case studies operational preparation and does not prescribe appointment lengths or medication-signing policy.

| Item | Meaning and limits | Consistent use |
|---|---|---|
| 60, 45, 20, 50, 40 visits/day | Check-in, triage, doctor, pharmacy and follow-up capacities in the initial serial illustration | Initial modeled output is 20; raising only check-in cannot change it |
| 60 arrival attempts, 20 completed visits | A representative working day; repeated attempts may involve the same person | Forty unsuccessful attempts, not forty unique people; no claim all arrive after opening |
| 250 working days/year | Explicit annualization assumption | 40 × 250 = 10,000 unsuccessful visit attempts; 6 × 250 = 1,500 additional completed visits if the projection holds |
| 8-hour day, 6 clinical hours, 20 consultations | Clinical time includes the history/assessment within consultations; remaining two hours are locating records, printing and other preparation | 360 / 20 = exactly 18 minutes; avoid counting repeated history both inside and outside the six hours |

## Product and evidence boundaries

The skill is an installed package for an AI assistant; keeping and reopening the same actual working file enables continuity. It is not automatically synchronized between arbitrary devices or independent copies. Phone chat remains an illustration of a prototype surface, with colleague invitations and explicit invocation. No patient records or identifying patient details appear in the example.

The execution record must contain real actions taken in this revision, not fabricated human dialogue. The public example is separate from the private collaboration file. All assistant-authored reasoning stays proposed unless a human explicitly endorses it. Schema validation checks structure and references, not truth. Reading the saved state in a fresh process checks persistence, not reliable behavior in a fresh model session or a successful real-world clinic intervention.
